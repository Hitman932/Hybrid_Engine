Install HybridEngine Studio as an App

Step 1: Click ⬇ Download App (.html) to save HybridEngine_Studio.html to your Chromebook.

Step 2: Open the downloaded file in Chrome, click the ⋮ menu (top right) → Cast, save, and share → Create shortcut...

Step 3: Check "Open as window" and click Create. HybridEngine Studio will now open in its own standalone window with an icon on your Chromebook shelf!
